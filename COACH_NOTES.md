# Coach Notes, Analysis & Priorities

This is a dated decision log of coaching observations, hypotheses, priorities and review points. Reassess these as new evidence arrives; do not treat hypotheses as established facts.

## 2026-11-23 — Initial shooting and 1v1 review

### Observations / evidence
- Midrange C&S: 12/30 (40%) in this session; results varied substantially by spot.
- 3PT C&S: 18/60 (30%) provisionally; round 2's last location is ambiguous.
- Pull-up shots: 13/24 (54.2%), but no drill-level breakdown.
- Catch preparation: user reports catching, adjusting feet, then shooting rather than flowing directly into shot. This suggests readiness/footwork before catch is a useful coaching target.
- Pull-up balance is improving subjectively but remains inconsistent.
- Finishing in informal 1v1 was variable; contested layup 2/4 and paint jump/floater-like finish 1/3. User reports shots sometimes strike side of backboard/rim.
- No evidence that simply jumping higher will fix these finishes. Evaluate release height, trajectory, touch, angle, ball protection, balance and decision/read.
- Shooting arm/elbow geometry: avoid rigidly imposing a 90-degree elbow; prioritize comfortable repeatable alignment and release.

### Provisional priorities (adjust with game evidence)
1. Catch readiness and catch-to-shot continuity: foot preparation, hand target, body alignment, rhythm.
2. Pull-up stability: deceleration, balance, repeatable shot setup off one dribble and hesitation.
3. Finishing: angle, soft touch, ball protection and choices under pressure; gradual safe contact progressions.
4. Three-point shooting: mechanics and repeatable distance, gradually build range without sacrificing technique.
5. Maintain floater/jump finish skill and gather options.
6. Continue gathering baseline data on playmaking, ball handling, defense and athleticism; do not neglect these or assume the shooting priorities are the only weaknesses.

### Next coaching decisions
- Vary next individual workout: game-like attacking sequence (read/penetrate/pull-up/finish) rather than repeating the same large stationary shooting volume.
- Make shot-preparation drills purposeful and track quality/accuracy; do not demand artificial speed.
- Introduce 3PT volume progressively and stop/reduce range when mechanics deteriorate.
- Keep workload appropriate to club sessions and game schedule; account for sleep, fatigue and ankle symptoms.
- Develop standardized retests and game log data before declaring progress.

### Data limitations
- All practice percentages are samples with different protocols and fatigue; do not compare directly as if conditions were identical.
- 1v1 and SKS observations are self-reported and context dependent.
- No video-based analysis yet.
