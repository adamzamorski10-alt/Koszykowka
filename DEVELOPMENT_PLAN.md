# Development Plan & Coach Roadmap

## Outcome goals
- Short-term performance goal: score 10+ points in a league game.
- Stretch performance goal: score 20 points in a league game.
- Broader goal: become a well-rounded impactful player—scoring, playmaking, defense, decision-making and athletic development.
These are goals, not promises or time-bound predictions.

## Current phase — Evidence gathering + foundational skill improvement
Priority areas currently hypothesized from initial data:
1. Catch-and-shoot preparation and flow.
2. Balanced/repeatable pull-up shooting.
3. Finishing under pressure (angles, touch, ball protection, timing).
4. Progressive three-point range and consistency.
5. Ongoing evaluation of handle, playmaking, defense and athletic qualities.

## Tracking and review loop
- Log each reported individual session and meaningful club/SKS/game observations in TRAINING_LOG.
- Store comparable test results and skill-specific numbers in SKILL_METRICS.
- Store coaching interpretations, hypotheses, and decisions in COACH_NOTES.
- Keep the profile and current plan updated as evidence changes.
- Review priorities periodically, and after games when available.
- Record game stats/context where possible: minutes, points, field goals by type, FT, turnovers, assists, rebounds, defensive matchups/events, role, game result and observations. Do not invent missing stats.

## Athlete reporting
For individual sessions: exercises, attempt/make numbers, effort (rough RPE if comfortable), fatigue, what felt/failed and any pain.
For games: minutes and stats if known; describe possessions or situations rather than only final scoring; report role and opponent context.
